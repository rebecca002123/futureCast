# Shift Pay — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 21 August 2026**

Shift Pay ("the app") records the shifts you work and works out what you
should be paid for them. This policy explains what information the app uses
and what happens to it. The short version: **Shift Pay collects nothing,
sends nothing anywhere, and has no servers of its own.**

## Information the app uses

### Data stored on your device

The shifts you enter — dates, times, breaks, notes — along with your hourly
rates and settings are stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private
storage. They are never uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone.
There is no account and no sign-in, so there is nothing to link them to you.
Deleting the app deletes them. "Delete all shifts" in Settings does the same
thing without removing the app.

### The network

The app makes **no network requests at all**. Every figure it shows — pay,
overtime, holiday accrual, the tax and National Insurance estimate — is
worked out on the device from the rates and rules you enter. It works with
the phone in aeroplane mode.

### Sharing

If you tap **Share summary** or **Share as CSV**, the app hands that text to
your phone's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent
anywhere until you pick a destination, and the app has no idea what you pick.

## What the app does NOT do

- No analytics or telemetry.
- No advertising, ad identifiers, or ad networks.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location access.
- No accounts, emails, passwords, or profiles.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of any personal information — we never have
  it in the first place.

## Accuracy

The pay and take-home figures are estimates produced from what you tell the
app. They are not payslips and are not tax advice. Always check them against
what your employer pays you.

## Children

Shift Pay does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. The app is rated 4+.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, this page
will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version
is released.
