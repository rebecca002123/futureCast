FutureCast

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026

FutureCast ("the app") is a weather forecast app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: FutureCast collects nothing, tracks nothing, and has no servers of its own.

Information the app uses

Location

FutureCast asks for access to your location only while you are using the app ("When In Use"). Your coordinates are used for exactly one purpose: they are sent to Apple Weather (Apple's WeatherKit service) to fetch the forecast for where you are.

  • Your location is never stored on any server operated by us — the app has no servers.
  • Your location is not linked to your identity. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your location is not used for tracking and is never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • You can also use the app entirely without location access by searching for a place by name. Place names you search are sent to Apple's geocoding service solely to find that place's coordinates.

Apple's handling of WeatherKit requests is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your saved places, settings, and cached forecasts are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on weather-change alerts, they are local notifications scheduled on your own device. No notification content passes through any third-party service.

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or ad networks.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No accounts, emails, passwords, or profiles.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any personal information — we never have it in the first place.

Children

FutureCast does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
